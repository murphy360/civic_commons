# Persona: The Retired Civic Watchdog

## Profile
**Name:** Harold "Hal" Morrison  
**Age:** 71  
**Occupation:** Retired accountant, now volunteer board member at Historical Society  
**Tech Comfort:** Moderate — uses email, basic web browsing, struggles with "modern" apps  
**Time Available:** Abundant — attends most public meetings in person

## Backstory
Hal has lived in Twinsburg for 45 years. He raised his kids here, served on the zoning board in the 90s, and now considers himself an unofficial guardian of local democracy. He attends city council meetings, reads every page of meeting minutes, and writes letters to the editor. He's frustrated that younger residents don't seem to care about local government.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "Track every decision"
- Reads full meeting minutes, not summaries
- Wants to see how council members voted on each issue
- Cross-references current decisions with historical context

### 2. "Research a topic over time"
- "What has the city said about the proposed development on Ravenna Road over the past 2 years?"
- Needs full-text search across all documents
- Values direct quotes and citations

### 3. "Prepare for meetings"
- Reviews agendas before attending
- Brings printed copies of relevant past minutes
- Wants to see linked documents (staff reports, proposals)

### 4. "Share with neighbors"
- Emails links to specific documents to his neighborhood email list
- Posts summaries on Nextdoor
- Needs shareable, permanent URLs

## Pain Points with Current System
- "The city website search is terrible — I can never find old minutes"
- "PDFs aren't searchable, I have to read the whole thing"
- "I know they discussed this 3 years ago, but I can't prove it"

## What Would Delight Hal
- Powerful full-text search across all historical documents
- Permanent links to specific meetings and documents
- Ability to see "related" documents (same topic, same project)
- Print-friendly versions

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Full-text search of PDF summaries — exactly what he needs
- Document storage with markdown extraction — searchable!
- Historical data preserved, not just upcoming events
- MCP search tool for natural language queries

### 👎 Concerns
- Will summaries replace full documents? He wants BOTH
- "content_markdown" — is the full text preserved or just summaries?
- No mention of vote tracking or roll calls
- How far back does historical data go?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add vote/roll call tracking to events table
- Keep original PDFs downloadable alongside extracted text
- Add "related documents" linking (same project/topic across meetings)
- Citation feature: "Link to this exact passage"
- Public archive that doesn't require login

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. Is full document text preserved, or just AI summaries?
2. How will historical documents be imported (backfill)?
3. Can he trust the AI summaries, or will he need to verify against originals?
4. What's the data retention policy?
