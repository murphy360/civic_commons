# Persona: The City Staff Member

## Profile
**Name:** Amanda Foster  
**Age:** 41  
**Occupation:** Assistant to the City Manager  
**Tech Comfort:** Medium-high — uses municipal software daily  
**Time Available:** Limited — handles many responsibilities

## Backstory
Amanda is the person who actually makes city government run. She prepares agendas, uploads documents to the city website, responds to resident inquiries, and supports the city manager. She's the one who has to answer "Where do I find X?" calls. She's interested in tools that reduce her workload but wary of things that create new problems.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "Answer resident questions faster"
- Frequent calls: "When is the next council meeting?"
- "Where do I find the minutes from last month?"
- "What did the city decide about X?"

### 2. "Understand what other departments are doing"
- Parks Dept announced something she didn't know about
- School board decision affects city operations
- Needs awareness without adding to her meeting load

### 3. "Verify information is correct"
- If this tool shows city data, is it accurate?
- Who fixes it if it's wrong?
- She'll get blamed if residents see incorrect info

### 4. "Reduce repetitive work"
- Same questions over and over
- Could a tool handle basic inquiries?
- Frees her time for complex issues

## Pain Points with Current System
- "I upload documents to our website, then answer questions about them anyway"
- "Residents can't find things on our website even though they're there"
- "I have no idea what the school board or parks dept is doing"
- "I spend hours on the phone with the same basic questions"

## What Would Delight Amanda
- Citizens self-serving basic questions
- Accurate information she can trust
- Clear process if something is wrong
- Cross-entity visibility without extra work

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Aggregates city + school + parks — useful for her too
- Public-facing tool means fewer phone calls
- Search functionality helps residents self-serve
- Admin dashboard for operators

### 👎 Concerns
- Data is scraped from website — what if she updates the website and scraper hasn't run?
- No way for city staff to correct information
- Will this compete with or complement the city website?
- Who is responsible when information is outdated/wrong?
- Is there a way to flag content for review?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add "data as of" timestamps prominently displayed
- Allow staff to trigger manual scrape after updates
- Create feedback mechanism: "Report incorrect information"
- Clarify relationship to official city website
- Consider staff read-only access to admin dashboard
- Add "Official Source" links back to city website
- Document data freshness SLAs (how quickly updates appear)

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. How quickly do website updates appear in Civic Commons?
2. Can staff trigger a manual refresh?
3. How are errors or outdated information corrected?
4. Does this replace or complement the city website?
5. What's the liability if information is incorrect?
