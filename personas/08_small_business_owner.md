# Persona: The Small Business Owner

## Profile
**Name:** Mike Patel  
**Age:** 48  
**Occupation:** Owns a restaurant on Main Street, active in Chamber of Commerce  
**Tech Comfort:** Medium — uses Square, social media for business, basic web skills  
**Time Available:** Almost none — works 70+ hours/week

## Backstory
Mike has owned his restaurant for 12 years. He's survived COVID, construction on Main Street, and three lease renewals. He knows that city decisions directly impact his business (parking, zoning, events that bring foot traffic) but he never has time to attend meetings. He finds out about changes after they happen and it's too late to have input.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "What's affecting my business?"
- Zoning changes near his location
- Parking regulations and enforcement
- Road construction and closures
- Special events that could bring customers

### 2. "Opportunities to participate"
- When is public comment on the streetscape project?
- Deadline for festival vendor applications
- Chamber events and networking

### 3. "Quick updates while I work"
- Check on phone between dinner rushes
- 2-minute scan of what's relevant
- "Anything about downtown businesses this week?"

### 4. "Connect with other business owners"
- What are other businesses saying about the new ordinance?
- Is the Chamber taking a position?
- Are there grants or programs he's missing?

## Pain Points with Current System
- "By the time I hear about something, the decision is already made"
- "I don't have time to attend 7pm meetings"
- "I didn't know I needed a permit for that"
- "The city never promotes local businesses"

## What Would Delight Mike
- Alerts for his specific interests (downtown, restaurants, parking)
- Deadlines clearly displayed (not buried in meeting minutes)
- Quick-scan mobile interface
- Business-relevant filter or category

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- All city info in one place
- Chamber of Commerce included as optional source
- MCP for quick questions — "Any road closures this week?"
- Mobile-responsive design

### 👎 Concerns
- No business-specific view or filtering
- Deadlines aren't a first-class concept (buried in events/documents)
- No mention of zoning or permit information
- How does he filter to just "things that affect downtown"?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add "Business Owner" view with relevant filters
- Extract and highlight DEADLINES as separate data type
- Add location-based filtering (downtown, specific ward)
- Include permit/license information if available
- Partner with Chamber to promote tool to business community
- Add economic development / business grants as a source

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. Can users filter by geographic area (downtown, ward, neighborhood)?
2. Are deadlines extracted and surfaced prominently?
3. Is permit/licensing information in scope?
4. How will business owners discover this tool?
5. Could this integrate with business-specific notifications?
