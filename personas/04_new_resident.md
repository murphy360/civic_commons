# Persona: The New Resident

## Profile
**Name:** Jordan Rivera  
**Age:** 29  
**Occupation:** Software Engineer (works in Cleveland, lives in Twinsburg)  
**Family:** Recently married, no kids yet, just bought first home  
**Tech Comfort:** Very high — expects modern UX, uses AI tools daily  
**Time Available:** Moderate — interested but not deeply invested yet

## Backstory
Jordan moved from Columbus 6 months ago for a job. They chose Twinsburg because it seemed like a nice suburb with good schools (for future planning). They don't know anyone on city council, have no idea how local government works, and feel disconnected from the community. They want to get involved but don't know where to start.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "How does this town work?"
- Basic orientation: What boards/entities exist?
- Who makes decisions about what?
- When and where are public meetings?

### 2. "What's the vibe here?"
- What issues are people talking about?
- Are there controversies? What are the sides?
- Is this a progressive or conservative community?

### 3. "Local events to meet people"
- Community events, festivals, farmers markets
- Not just government meetings — fun stuff
- Metroparks events, library programs

### 4. "Did I miss anything important?"
- Catch-up mode: "What happened in local government this month?"
- Doesn't want to read minutes — wants summaries
- Asks AI: "Anything I should know as a new homeowner?"

## Pain Points with Current System
- "I don't even know what websites to check"
- "Government websites feel like they're from 2005"
- "I don't know the difference between city council and township trustees"
- "Meeting minutes assume I know the backstory"

## What Would Delight Jordan
- Clean, modern interface (not government-website-ugly)
- "New resident" onboarding: Here's how Twinsburg works
- AI that can explain context, not just retrieve documents
- Event discovery for social/community activities

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Modern tech stack (Next.js, Tailwind) — will look good
- LLM interface — natural way to explore unfamiliar territory
- Aggregates everything in one place
- Mobile-friendly mentioned

### 👎 Concerns
- No onboarding flow for new residents
- Assumes users know what sources exist
- Historical Society, Parks, Library are included — great! But how do they discover them?
- AI summaries assume context — will a newcomer understand?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add "New to Twinsburg?" landing page or flow
- Explain what each source IS (City Council ≠ School Board)
- "Explain like I'm new" mode for AI responses
- Community events calendar (not just government)
- Add tags: "Community", "Government", "Family", "Outdoors"

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. How does someone discover this tool when they move to town?
2. Can the AI explain context, or does it assume local knowledge?
3. Is this just government, or broader community?
4. Could this be THE place for Twinsburg info?
