# Persona: The Busy Parent

## Profile
**Name:** Sarah Chen  
**Age:** 38  
**Occupation:** Marketing Manager (remote)  
**Family:** Married, two kids (ages 6 and 10)  
**Tech Comfort:** High — uses apps daily, expects things to "just work"  
**Time Available:** Very limited — usually checking phone between activities

## Backstory
Sarah moved to Twinsburg 4 years ago for the schools. She's constantly juggling work calls, soccer practice, and homework help. She wants to be an informed citizen but doesn't have time to check 5 different websites. She missed the school levy vote last year because she didn't know it was happening until the day before.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "What's happening at the schools?"
- Wants school board meeting summaries, not full minutes
- Cares about: budget decisions, curriculum changes, school calendar
- Needs: Push notifications or weekly digest for school-related items

### 2. "Summer activities for the kids"
- Searches for parks & rec programs, library events, metroparks activities
- Filters by: age-appropriate, date range, location
- Wants: Direct links to registration

### 3. "Quick catch-up on city stuff"
- 5-minute weekly summary of what happened in local government
- Prefers: Bullet points, not full documents
- Asks LLM: "Anything important from city council this week?"

## Pain Points with Current System
- "I have to check the city website, school website, library website, parks website..."
- "Meeting minutes are 40-page PDFs — I don't have time for that"
- "I only find out about things after they've already been decided"

## What Would Delight Sarah
- Mobile-first calendar view with filters
- AI-generated summaries: "3 things parents should know from this week"
- "Subscribe" to topics (schools, kids activities) for notifications
- Quick answer to "When is the next school board meeting?"

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Unified calendar across all sources — huge time saver
- MCP for LLM queries — "just ask" is perfect for busy lifestyle
- Mobile-responsive design mentioned

### 👎 Concerns
- No mention of notifications/alerts — will she have to keep checking?
- PDF summaries are great, but are they parent-friendly or bureaucratic?
- How does she filter to just kid-relevant stuff?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add topic tags to events (education, kids, parks, government)
- Weekly email digest option
- "Family mode" filter that shows kid-friendly events
- Integration with Google Calendar / Apple Calendar for export

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. How will Sarah discover this tool exists?
2. What's the 30-second value proposition for her?
3. Can she get value without creating an account?
