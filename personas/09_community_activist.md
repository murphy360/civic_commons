# Persona: The Community Activist

## Profile
**Name:** Keisha Washington  
**Age:** 35  
**Occupation:** Social worker, leads local advocacy group  
**Tech Comfort:** High — organizes via social media, email lists, petition sites  
**Time Available:** Dedicated — this is her passion alongside her job

## Backstory
Keisha started a neighborhood group 3 years ago to address traffic safety near the elementary school. Since then, she's become known as someone who shows up to meetings, asks hard questions, and mobilizes neighbors. She's learned how local government works through trial and error, and wants to help others get involved without the steep learning curve.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "Track an issue across time"
- Follow a specific topic (traffic safety, housing, police budget)
- See every mention across all meetings and documents
- Build a timeline for advocacy campaigns

### 2. "Mobilize for public comment"
- When is the hearing on the traffic study?
- How do people sign up to speak?
- Share meeting details with her network quickly

### 3. "Hold officials accountable"
- What did they promise last year?
- How did each council member vote?
- Find direct quotes for social media posts

### 4. "Train new advocates"
- Help newcomers understand how local government works
- Show them where to find information
- Reduce the "insider knowledge" barrier

## Pain Points with Current System
- "I have to piece together information from 10 sources"
- "I can never find what was said at that meeting 6 months ago"
- "New people give up because it's too hard to follow"
- "Officials say one thing and vote another — hard to prove"

## What Would Delight Keisha
- Topic tracking across all sources and time
- Vote tracking by individual official
- Shareable links to specific quotes/decisions
- Public comment deadlines prominently displayed
- Tool to help onboard new community members

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Full-text search across all documents — essential for research
- MCP for natural language queries — "What has council said about traffic on Ravenna Road?"
- Historical data preserved
- Open source — community could contribute

### 👎 Concerns
- No vote tracking by individual (schema has events, not votes)
- No topic/issue tracking over time
- Public comment opportunities not highlighted
- How does she share a specific finding? Permanent links?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add vote/roll call tracking: Who voted yes/no on what
- Add "topic" or "issue" tagging that persists across meetings
- Highlight public comment opportunities and deadlines
- Create shareable, embeddable links to specific documents/quotes
- Add "advocacy toolkit" — how to get involved guide
- Consider notification system for tracked topics

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. Is vote tracking (by individual official) in scope?
2. Can topics be tracked across meetings over time?
3. Are public comment opportunities surfaced separately?
4. Can findings be shared with permanent links?
5. Is there an onboarding guide for civic newcomers?
