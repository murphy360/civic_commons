# Persona: The City Council Member

## Profile
**Name:** Marcus Thompson  
**Age:** 52  
**Occupation:** City Council Member (part-time), owns local insurance agency  
**Tech Comfort:** Medium — uses business software, smartphone, but not a "tech person"  
**Time Available:** Limited — council is a part-time civic duty

## Backstory
Marcus was elected 3 years ago after getting involved in a neighborhood traffic issue. He genuinely wants to serve the community but is overwhelmed by the volume of information. He gets complaints from residents about things he's never heard of, and often the school board or parks department made the decision — not city council. He wishes residents understood the difference.

## Primary Use Cases

### 1. "What are residents asking about?"
- Wants to see what questions the LLM is being asked
- Identifies trending topics and concerns
- Prepares for constituent questions

### 2. "What are other boards doing?"
- Stays informed on school board, parks, library decisions
- Avoids surprises when residents assume "the city" made a decision
- Coordinates on overlapping issues (traffic near schools, park funding)

### 3. "Quick answer for a resident"
- Constituent calls: "Why is the library closed on Mondays?"
- Needs fast lookup across all civic entities
- Can forward accurate information without researching manually

### 4. "Meeting preparation"
- Reviews past decisions on upcoming agenda items
- Sees full history of a project/issue
- Understands what staff reports are saying

## Pain Points with Current System
- "Residents think I control everything — I only vote on city matters"
- "I find out about school board decisions from angry parents"
- "I can't remember what we decided 6 months ago on this issue"

## What Would Delight Marcus
- Cross-entity awareness: "What did the school board decide about X?"
- Query analytics: "What are residents asking about?"
- Quick lookup for constituent questions
- Mobile access during community events

## Feedback on Current Design

### 👍 Likes
- Aggregation across entities — finally sees the full picture
- MCP interface — could potentially integrate with his workflow
- Event calendar across all sources
- Search across all documents

### 👎 Concerns
- No analytics on usage/queries — can't see what residents care about
- Is this a tool FOR government or ABOUT government?
- How does official information flow? Can city update their own data?
- Privacy: Are resident queries logged? Should they be?

### 💡 Suggestions
- Add optional query analytics for admins (anonymized)
- Clarify data flow: Is this pull-only, or can entities push updates?
- Consider "official response" feature for common questions
- Add "jurisdiction" labels: "This is a school board matter, not city council"
- Provide council members view-only admin access

## Key Questions for This Persona
1. Is this tool for citizens only, or also for government officials?
2. Will the city need to maintain anything, or is it fully automated?
3. How does incorrect information get corrected?
4. Could this reduce public records requests?
